# English item-text devices

Corpus: 28 official EN descriptions across DS1/DS2/DS3, Bloodborne, Sekiro, Elden Ring + Shadow of the Erdtree. Quotes are calibration only; never reproduce them in output.

## Openers

- Article-less noun-phrase fragment. `[Type] of [owner/place].` or `[Adj] [type] [participle clause].` No verb, no "A", no "This is".
  - "Soul of a long-lost Fire Keeper." (DS1) · "Sacred sword of Rykard, Lord of Blasphemy." (ER) · "Skull of a madman touched by the wisdom of the Great Ones." (BB)
- "One of the …" set-membership. Places the object inside a catalogue the reader never sees.
  - "One of the trick weapons of the workshop, commonly used in the hunting business." (BB) · "One of the incantations of the capital's ancient dragon cult." (ER)
- Appositive name-tag. Proper noun, comma, title. Never explained.
  - "Greatsword of Drummond, Royal Army Captain." (DS2) · "Rennala, Queen of the Full Moon" (ER)

## The mechanical sentence

- Deadpan effect line, 3-6 words, present tense, sitting inside the text right after the name-line. No numbers.
  - "Fill with Estus at bonfire. Fills HP." (DS1) · "A medallion with Crimson amber inlaid. Boosts maximum HP." (ER) · "Use to gain Insight." (BB) · "Hurl giant chaos fireball." (DS1)
- Boss-soul boilerplate. "Use to acquire numerous souls, or transpose to extract its true strength." (DS3) · "Alternatively, it can be used to gain a great bounty of runes." (ER)
- Skill footer, after the lore. `Skill: [Name]` or `Unique Skill: [Name]`, newline, one imperative instruction sentence.
  - "Skill: Stance / While in stance, use normal attack to break a foe's guard from below…" (DS3) · "Unique Skill: Taker's Flames / Raise the sacred sword aloft…" (ER)

## Tense and time

- Past origin → present remnant. Origin in past/perfect, then a present-tense sentence about what persists: `still`, `long since`, `to this day`, `even now`, `now`.
  - "This old ring is named for its decorative green blossom, but its luster is long since faded." (DS3) · "…it appears to have otherwise been kept in a serviceable condition, despite the soldiers having long since lost their minds." (ER)
- One-sentence myth. "Long ago, …" / "When …, …".
  - "Long ago, Godwyn the Golden defeated the ancient dragon Fortissax, and befriended his fallen foe" (ER) · "When the carcass of Kos washed up on the coast, its insides were teeming with tiny parasites" (BB)

## Understatement

- Concessive adverbs: `perhaps`, `somewhat`, `rather`, `quite`, `slightly`, `just possible`, `otherwise`, `if gradual`, `little more than`, `of no particular`.
  - "An old and unadorned sword, perhaps, but the pride and joy of the venerable captain." (DS2) · "despite the inevitable, if gradual, putrefaction of their flesh" (ER) · "It is just possible to make out the majestic gold engravings" (DS3)
- Praise, then deflate in the same breath.
  - "The Knights of Lothric, with their drakes, once crushed anything that threatened their shores. Of course, that was a long, long time ago." (DS3)

## Closers

- Pivot word opens the last sentence: `Alas`, `Of course`, `No surprise`, `Perhaps`, `Now`, `All so`, `In turn`, `Quite`, `Yet`, `But`, `Only`.
  - "Alas, this ring will not make you human. What is lost is not easily retrieved." (DS2) · "No surprise that most Yharnamites are heavy users of blood." (BB) · "All so he would never have to abandon his beloved but scrawny steed." (ER) · "Quite befitting of an unkindled, an Ashen Estus Flask turns a bonfire's heat cold." (DS3)
- Unanswered question. "Was … or …?"
  - "Was the Ash Maiden locked in this dark prison for some transgression, or by her own will?" (DS1)
- Verdict fragment. 2-4 words, no verb or a bare one.
  - "Imperfect beings, each and all." (ER) · "The incessant coughing must cease." (Sekiro)
- Aphorism as cost. General truth in present tense.
  - "Making contact with eldritch wisdom is a blessing, for even if it drives one mad, it allows one to serve a grander purpose, for posterity." (BB)

## Attribution beyond "it is said"

`was said to have been felled by an unknown warrior` (DS2) · `known in legend to possess "fateful beauty"` (DS1, scare-quoted epithet) · `The Dark Tales also make reference:` + quoted verse (DS1) · `remains a mystery` (DS2) · `celebrated for their undefeated campaign` (ER) · `…or so it is said` (ER).

## Names

`Owner's + Type` (Lordsworn's Straight Sword, Milady) · `Type + of + Title` (Remembrance of the Starscourge, Soul of the Lords) · `Place + Type` (Heide Knight Sword, Drangleic Sword) · one compound adjective (Blasphemous Blade, Cleanrot Helm) · possessive body-part (Kos Parasite, Madman's Knowledge). Capitalised concepts stay unglossed: First Flame, Age of the Erdtree, Shattering, Great Ones, Fountainhead.

## Per-game dial

| Game | Shape | Lexicon and tics |
|---|---|---|
| DS1 | 2 blocks, 1-2 sentences each; blunt, sometimes ungrammatical ("Hurl giant chaos fireball.") | humanity, Anor Londo, bosoms, writhe; verse quotes |
| DS2 | 2-3 blocks, 2 sentences each; elegiac | unknown warrior, none remember, remains a mystery, Alas, what is lost |
| DS3 | 2-3 blocks; short; wry | long since, unkindled, ash, "that was a long, long time ago", `Skill:` footer |
| BB | 1-3 blocks; longer clauses | workshop, hunting business, beasts, blood ministration, Yharnamites, Great Ones, eldritch, No surprise, Surely |
| Sekiro | 1 block, 3-5 sentences, procedural | Fountainhead, Senpou Temple, blessing, karma, Sculptor's Idol; ends on a terse moral |
| ER | 3 blocks: object+effect / rite or origin / cost; 1-2 sentences each | Erdtree, grace, runes, Shattering, demigod, It is said that…, `Unique Skill:` |

## Tells of a generic imitation

1. Opens with "A …" or "This is a …" instead of a fragment.
2. Effect line missing, or parked in a stats footer instead of 3-6 words after the name-line.
3. Solemn throughout; no `perhaps`, `somewhat`, `of course`, `alas`, `no surprise`.
4. Ends on mood ("…and the darkness remembers") instead of a cost, a question, or a verdict fragment.
5. Only "it is said"; none of `known in legend`, `remains a mystery`, `or so it is said`.
6. One tense throughout; no `long since` / `still` / `now` pivot.
7. Names like "X of the Y-touched Z"; real names are plain possessives or one compound adjective.
8. Weapon card without a `Skill:` footer.
