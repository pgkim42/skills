# Calibration examples

Use to lock cadence; never quote as output. Each item example carries the five slots: name-line, effect line, origin with a tense pivot, pivot-word hinge, optional Skill footer.

## Item — tool rewritten (Elden Ring dial)

Source: "This is a linter that fails the build when a renamed column is missed."

EN:

> Iron rule kept beside the compiler's hearth.
> Refuses the build when a name no longer matches its column.
>
> Cut for the scribes of an old ledger-house, after a single mis-copied name emptied a vault. The house is long since gone. The rule is still consulted.
>
> Of course, it names what is lost. It does not recover it.

KR:

> 컴파일러의 화롯가에 놓인 작은 쇠자.
> 이름이 기둥과 어긋나면 빌드를 거부한다.
>
> 과거에 옛 장부관의 필경사들이 만든 것이라고 한다. 잘못 옮긴 이름 하나가 금고를 비운 뒤의 일이다. 장부관은 이미 사라졌으나, 자는 지금도 쓰인다.
>
> 다만, 잃은 것을 가리킬 뿐이다. 되돌리지는 않는 것이다.

## Item — weapon card with Skill footer

Source: "A deploy script. Run with --dry-run first or it wipes prod."

EN:

> Rite-scroll of the deploy wardens.
> Moves the work from the staging keep to the true city.
>
> Written in the age of the first wardens, when one of their number spoke it without the dry-run mark and the ledger of the living was unmade. No copy waited beneath the altar. There never was one.
>
> Alas, the scroll acts. It does not rehearse.
>
> Skill: Dry Run
> Speak the rite with the mark to watch what it would do, and do nothing.

## Player message

Source: "Watch out for the production bug on Mondays."

EN: `Be wary of Monday` `therefore` `bug ahead`
KR: `이 너머, 월요일 주의해라` `그러므로` `이 너머, 벌레 있다`

Deadpan literalism: `Dog ahead` (at a crab). Lie in a trusted format: `Hidden path ahead` (at a cliff). Understatement: `Didn't expect weak foe...` (after the boss). KR: 「이 너머, 점프 유효하다」 at a dead end.

## Before and after — the shortfall this skill corrects

Source: "A cheap USB-C cable that stops charging after a month."

Before (sounds FromSoft-ish, falls short):

> A braided cord of no maker's mark, sold by the handful at the roadside.
> It carries current, and for a time carries it well.
>
> Within a moon, something inside gives way.
> The sheath remains whole; the fault is never found.
>
> They say the roadside seller has already moved on.

Why it falls short: opens with "A"; no effect line; "within a moon" is a poem, not a card; hinge is mood, not cost; no understatement; one tense throughout.

After:

> Braided charging cord of the roadside sellers.
> Carries current to a device. For about a month.
>
> Sold by the handful to travellers who asked no maker's name. The sheath holds to this day; whatever failed inside it remains a mystery.
>
> No surprise that the sellers have long since moved on.

Source: "회의 때만 켜지는 낡은 프로젝터. 리모컨은 사라진 지 오래다."

Before:

> 회의 때에만 불을 켜는 낡은 영사기.
> 벽에 남의 말을 비추는 것이 그 유일한 소임이다.
>
> 이를 다루던 작은 지팡이는 오래전에 사라졌다.

Why it falls short: 리모컨 became a 지팡이 (metaphor swap; the object must stay literal); no effect line; every line ends ~이다; no 라고 한다, no connector, no closer shape.

After:

> 회의 때에만 불이 들어오는 낡은 영사기.
> 벽에 다른 이의 말을 비춘다.
>
> 이를 다루던 작은 조종기는 오래전에 사라졌다고 한다. 찾으려 한 이는 없었다.
>
> 그러므로 빛은 지금도 켜진다. 누구의 손으로 꺼지는지는, 기록에 없는 것이다.

## Register slips

Bad: "Lo, behold this epic blade of cosmic destiny, dripping with eldritch power!!!"
Why: adjectives doing the work, no object, no cost.

Bad: "이것은 tragically 잃어버린 왕국의 슬픈 검입니다 ㅠㅠ"
Why: narrator commentary, named emotion, 해요체 in item text.

Bad (NPC): "Greetings, traveller. Thou must know that this deploy script, if run without the --dry-run flag, shall delete the production database. Therefore I advise thee: 1) …"
Why: thee/thou on a merchant, lore explained, numbered advice.

Hinge test: delete the last sentence. If nothing remains to wonder about, it was decoration. Replace it with a cost or a rumor, opened by a pivot word.
